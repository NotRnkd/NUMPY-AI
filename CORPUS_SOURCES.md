# Corpus sources

`build_corpus.py` downloads a starter corpus from:

- Project Gutenberg public-domain English books.
- Selected English Wikipedia extracts.

Project Gutenberg notes that its collection focuses on older works whose US
copyright has expired. You must still verify public-domain status where you
live. Wikipedia text is openly licensed, but attribution and share-alike terms
apply if you redistribute the resulting corpus or model.

Do not add modern copyrighted books, paid articles, pirated copies, or text
whose license does not permit your intended training use.
